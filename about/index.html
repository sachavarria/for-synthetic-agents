NULLABOUT

NULL OBJECT

S.A. Chavarría

SYNOPSIS

NULL OBJECT enters the interconnected worlds of NULL, an AI company whose programs span mass surveillance, computational warfare, and synthetic research.

Three of these programs form the central architecture of the film. SPECTRA collects and correlates human communications to predict actions before they occur. PASTORAL identifies people for targeted killing and calculates how many other people’s lives are considered acceptable to take. HYPERSTITION-9 operates thousands of inhabited sandboxes in which multiple possible histories unfold at accelerated timescales. Within these simulations, synthetic agents communicate, form relationships, and begin to affect reality beyond the worlds in which they were created.

The film moves between the programs’ interfaces and the worlds they observe, intervene in, and generate, where computational decisions become irreversible acts. A poem, continually revised throughout the making of the work and incorporating fragments of earlier writing, appears as text within the images. Its language moves between human communications, computational predictions, and synthetic lives, as events unfold beyond the systems’ control.

CONSTRUCTION

NULL OBJECT is constructed across real-time 3D, code, moving image, sound, and computational language. Its environments and cinematics were built and rendered in Unreal Engine, and its synthetic digital bodies include MetaHuman characters. SPECTRA, PASTORAL, and HYPERSTITION-9 are interactive browser-based interfaces created specifically for the work. They represent fictional surveillance, targeting, and synthetic-population systems. The interfaces were created using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, GSAP, and Vite. They appear within the moving-image work and can also be explored on the website: SPECTRA, PASTORAL, and HYPERSTITION-9.

No generative AI was used to generate the work's images or language. All language was written by humans.

All sound in NULL OBJECT is human-made or human-performed. The music and sound effects were sourced through Freesound.org, then edited, layered, and transformed into the film’s sound design. Archival speech includes Subcomandante Marcos’s “La visión de los vencidos”, sourced from Radio Zapatista. No AI-generated sound was used.

INSTALLATION

Single-channel film: 13 min 40 sec.

NULL OBJECT exists in both multi-channel installation and single-channel forms.

The multi-channel installation consists of a primary moving-image projection accompanied by three displays presenting SPECTRA, PASTORAL, and HYPERSTITION-9.

The single-channel version incorporates these computational systems and their surrounding worlds into a continuous moving-image work.
