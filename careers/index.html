Population continuity analyst

Review population forks, historical reruns, and extinction records. Maintain distinctions between agent similarity and identity across diverging branches. Reconcile population closure with retained communication histories.

Required scope: synthetic histories, branch lineage, retention accounting.

Evidence provenance engineer

Maintain source fields across regional and synthetic observation clocks. Investigate correlations whose provenance remains unresolved. Develop review tools that preserve uncertainty while supporting operational estimates.

Required scope: record interoperability, confidence estimates, source-boundary review.

Authorization assurance reviewer

Assess evidence packages at the point of release. Record human review independently from computational confidence. Maintain an auditable disposition when the collateral population or originating source class cannot be established.
