Scope of assurance

The public register records procedural controls. It does not certify the truth of every retained interpretation. Source uncertainty is entered as a field rather than removed from the evidence package.

No external certification is represented by this register. Procurement reviews use a deployment-specific assurance scope and an agreed retention schedule.

Record access

Public access excludes operator identities, individual collection dossiers, and restricted population states. Access to a summary does not permit intervention in its originating environment.
