Extinction describes the absence of continuing population states in the designated branch. It does not describe the absence of stored agents, communication records, or downstream interpretations.

Reinstatement

A rerun initiated from a retained checkpoint receives a new branch identifier. It is recorded as a new population history. The extinction record for the prior branch remains filed.

Reinstatement of a world does not rescind an authorization derived from its earlier records.
