Population and world ownership

Licensing assigns access to computational capacity and records. A synthetic population is administered within its allocated world. Agent continuity across forks is described by branch identifiers rather than transferred by implication.

License expiry

Expiry suspends new execution requests. Existing records follow the retention policy. Termination of an allocation does not recall evidence already accepted by SPECTRA or PASTORAL.

Cross-sandbox contamination

Licensees must preserve source identifiers when communicating between worlds. Unregistered recurrence of external language is entered as a condition record. Suspending a license does not close that condition.
